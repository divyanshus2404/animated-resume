Pay in 3, in motion

A motion study of the moments inside a buy-now-pay-later checkout: picking a plan, seeing the price split, paying, and tracking what's left. Every interaction on this page is live, and each one has its timing written down next to it.

The goal is calm, not flashy. Credit decisions make people nervous, so motion here has two jobs: show what just changed, and make "₹0 extra" feel believable.

Plan switcher

₹999
per month · ₹0 extra
Duration
500 ms knob, 450 ms number count
Easing
Spring, cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1)
Why
The slight overshoot says "selected". The number counts rather than snapping, so you can see the monthly amount go down as the plan gets longer.

Pay button

Duration
150 ms press, 400 ms morph, 450 ms tick draw
Easing
Ease-out, cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
Why
The button shrinks into its own loader, so you never wonder whether your tap registered. It turns into a green tick in the same spot, which reads as "done" with nothing else to look at.

Instalment tracker

Today30 days60 days
Duration
600 ms fill, then a 450 ms pop on the dot
Easing
Ease-out fill, spring pop
Why
Paying back should feel like progress. The line fills first and the dot pops only when it arrives, a small reward for each instalment.

Built by hand in HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript: no animation library, so every curve is visible in the source. Motion turns off automatically when your system asks for reduced motion.
