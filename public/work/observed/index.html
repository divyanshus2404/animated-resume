A head shake

A quick side-to-side head shake says "no, try again" before anyone says a word. It's understood instantly and it isn't rude.

Try any code, then try 2580
Duration
420 ms, four swings that get smaller
Easing
Ease-out decay, red border for 1 s
Why
A wrong OTP shows up as a gesture first and a message second. The boxes clear and focus jumps back to the first one, so trying again takes no extra tap.

Pushing a note aside

At the kirana counter, a reminder chit gets nudged to the side, not thrown away. It's still there, just not now.

Snooze 1 dayRemind tonight
Instalment due in 3 days₹999 for Court Runner sneakers
Input
Drag sideways, 35% of the card width to commit
Easing
350 ms fly-out; 450 ms spring back under the threshold
Why
Neither direction dismisses the reminder for good; both just push it to later. The label underneath shows what will happen before you let go.
Observed

The gullak

A clay piggy bank on a shelf. You can't see inside, but you hear every coin land, and that sound is the reward.

Saved with 0% EMI
₹0
Duration
550 ms coin arc, 500 ms level rise after it lands
Easing
Gravity curve on the drop, spring on the level
Why
Paying on time turns into a visible saving instead of money disappearing. The level only rises once the coin is inside, so cause and effect stay clear.
