Your limit, revealed

A motion study of a first-time user's onboarding: verifying identity, waiting for a decision, and the moment they find out how much they can spend.

That moment carries the most emotion in the whole app. So the motion slows down: the ring fills, the number counts up, and the card rises only after the number has landed.

Duration
450 ms line, then 300 ms dot fill
Easing
Ease-out, cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
Why
The line travels first and the next dot lights up only when it arrives. That reads as "you moved forward" instead of two things changing at once.

Limit ring

₹0limit
Duration
1400 ms ring and count, in sync
Easing
Ease-out cubic on both
Why
The longest animation in the flow, on purpose. Most of the number arrives fast, then it settles slowly, which gives the user a beat to take it in.

Card tilt

Credit limit₹25,000
Input
Pointer or finger position, ±10° on each axis
Easing
250 ms ease-out follow, springs flat on release
Why
Moving a highlight across the card makes it feel like an object you now own, not just a number on a screen. The tilt is capped so it never gets in the way of reading.
